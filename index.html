    Weekly sets per muscle

    Around 10 hard sets per muscle per week clearly beats fewer; more still helps a little for size, with diminishing returns, while strength plateaus earlier. Sets taken close to failure count more but also cost more recovery, so 8–12 is a practical range for most people. Synergist sets count half, stabiliser work is not counted.
